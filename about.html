About Us

Connecting talent with opportunity since 2009.

CNT Promo & Ads Specialists, Inc. is one of the Philippines’ most trusted manpower and staffing partners — pairing exceptional Filipino talent with the country’s leading organizations.

Our Story

Built on trust, driven by results.

For over 15 years, CNT has helped corporations across the Philippines build the teams that power their growth — from a single C-suite executive to full field forces of hundreds deployed across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

As a DOLE-licensed employment agency, we combine a deep talent network with disciplined, compliant processes: executive search, mass and bulk hiring, contractual staffing, HR outsourcing, background screening, and training. The result is a partner our clients rely on — and a launchpad for a million Filipino careers.
